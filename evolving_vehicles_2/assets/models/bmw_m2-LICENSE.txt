WebSim modifications:
- Decoded compressed and quantized data; removed the studio ground plane only.
- Preserved all 228,676 vehicle triangles in the main runtime model.
- Rotated and uniformly scaled the vehicle to 4.468 m length, with tire ground
  at zero. Original body, lamps, wheels and decorative red/blue livery retained.
- Preserved six original base-color textures; adapted glazing, lamp-cover,
  paint-reflection, light-guide and metal materials to the exterior renderer.
  Normal and metallic/roughness maps are not used by this renderer.
- Produced a separate simplified fallback with source-texture sampled colors.
- Compacted and grouped indexed geometry and embedded the data in a local,
  gzip-compressed JavaScript runtime. No newly generated body geometry added.
