The .geojson files are extractions of OSM raceway ways. The source response,
request, download timestamp, original way IDs, and derivation notes are supplied
in this directory so these derived geometries can be inspected and regenerated.
No ownership of the OpenStreetMap data is claimed by WebSim.

These data do not share the MIT license of the separate f1-circuits dataset.
Facility lengths and descriptions have their individual factual sources in
korean-circuits.metadata.json. Operator illustrations are not bundled or traced.
